I'm a Ph.D. candidate at Columbia University, working at the intersection of AI and chemical engineering. I previously graduated from Georgia Tech with a B.S. in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering and did research in computational molecular design (DFT/MD) for CO2 capture and storage.

My research is currently funded by the 🏆**NSF Graduate Research Fellowship**🏆 and in active collaboration with Aspen Technology.

#### 🔬 Research Projects

- **PSOFormer** reads the first ten iterations of an optimizer's search trajectory and forecasts its converged parameter vector in one forward pass. This transformer-based amortized optimization method transfers zero-shot to unseen ODE systems with 30-50x fewer simulator calls.

- **Self-Learning Evolutionary Algorithm (SLEA)** uses multi-armed bandits with KL-UCB to learn adaptive search strategies online for combinatorial optimization. This neuro-symbolic AI discovers the correct reaction network with ~6,800x fewer candidate evaluations than baseline algorithms.

- **Statistical Teleodynamics** is an econophysics framework that integrates game theoretic principles with statistical thermodynamics to model phase transitions in self-organizing systems. Current applications include developing a novel algorithm for autonomous drone swarms and modeling how an agent swarm's communication topology shapes its collective problem solving.
